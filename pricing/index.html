Common questions

Why per-device pricing?

It matches the bill to what we’re actually watching — every laptop, server, and workstation we monitor, patch, and protect. You can do the math in your head, it doesn’t balloon when someone hires a contractor, and it’s how most MSPs price these days.

Which tier should I pick?

Most people land on Managed IT + Security. It covers the whole IT department job and includes the security pieces every small business honestly needs at this point. Pick Core if you just want the plumbing handled, or Premium if you’re dealing with HIPAA, an audit, or you want someone sitting down with you every quarter to plan ahead.

What counts as a “device”?

Anything we actively manage: workstations, laptops, and servers. Phones and printers don’t count.

What if I’m under 10 devices?

Talk to us. We work with churches, non-profits, and smaller teams all the time. We’ll size something that fits without forcing you into a tier you don’t need.

Can we start small and grow?

Yep. A lot of folks start on Core or Managed IT + Security and move up once the business grows or an audit shows up on the calendar.
