Both are honest answers. It depends on how much of your IT is already quiet.

Frequently asked questions

What does break/fix IT support cost?

$75 an hour, flat, billed in 15-minute increments. No minimum charge and no retainer. Hardware is billed separately at cost, and never without your approval first.

Is there a contract or a monthly minimum?

Nope. No monthly fee to keep us on call, and no minimum hours. You pay for the work you ask for, and nothing in the months you don’t call.

How fast will you respond?

Hourly work is best-effort, usually the next business day. Managed clients come first — that’s part of what a plan buys them. We’d rather say that plainly than imply something we don’t offer.

Do you charge for travel?

Remote work carries no travel charge. We cover Washington County and the Mid-Ohio Valley from Beverly. On-site visits are quoted with the job before we head out.

Can we move to a monthly plan later?

Any time. If you find yourself calling every week, a flat per-device plan usually makes more sense — and the work we’ve already done doesn’t get thrown away.
