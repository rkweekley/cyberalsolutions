What's included

Managed IT Support & Help Desk

24/7 endpoint monitoring (RMM), patch management, workstation and server support, and same-day response when something breaks. Phone, email, or Messenger — whichever's easiest. The person who picks up is the person fixing it.

Managed Cybersecurity (MDR / SOC)

Endpoint detection & response, 24/7 SOC monitoring, vulnerability scanning, and security awareness training for your staff. The big-firm security stack, sized for organizations that just need things to work without a six-figure budget.

Email & Identity Protection

MFA rollout, phishing defense, Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace identity hardening, conditional access, and DMARC/SPF/DKIM tuning. The single most cost‑effective security investment a small business can make.

Backup & Recovery

Backups that get tested, not just scheduled. We follow the 3-2-1-1-0 rule with immutable copies and real restore drills, because a backup you've never tested isn't actually a backup.

Microsoft 365 & Cloud Administration

Tenant setup, license cleanup, secure file sharing, SharePoint & Teams admin, and the day-to-day cloud work that nobody on your team wants to own.

Network & Infrastructure Monitoring

Wired and wireless setups, switches, firewalls, VPN, and ongoing monitoring for offices, churches, shops, and clinics. We pick gear that'll still be supported in five years and document it so the next person isn't guessing.

IT Strategy & vCIO (Premium tier)

Quarterly strategy reviews, multi-year roadmaps, compliance assistance (HIPAA, audit readiness), and a budget that doesn't catch fire every December.
