What the review actually looks like

Most IT pitches start with a sales deck. This one doesn’t. We spend the time on your stuff, not ours.

1. A short conversation

Tell us how your team works, what’s been bugging you, and where you feel exposed. Five to ten minutes.

2. A real look

We walk through the basics with you: identity and MFA, backups, Microsoft 365 settings, and what’s sitting on your network.

3. A short list

You leave with three or four things we’d fix first, in plain language. You can do them yourself, or hire us. Either is fine.

A few honest questions

Is this really free?

Yes. 30 minutes, no charge. We do this because most of the time the conversation tells us whether we’re a fit, and it tells you whether you actually need outside help. Sometimes the answer is “you’re fine, here’s what to watch for.” That’s a good outcome too.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Just be ready to talk through how your team uses email, files, and any systems you depend on. If you have admin access to your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, that helps, but isn’t required.

Will you try to sell me a long contract?

No. We work with clients on month-to-month support and on one-off projects. If a long-term agreement makes sense for you, we’ll talk about it. If not, we won’t.

Can you come on-site?

If you’re in or near Washington County, Ohio, yes. Otherwise we do the review over a video call and screen share, which works better than people expect.
